We try to capture the key dynamics in a single head transformer with easiest set-up. The different clustered phase are discussed in detail and the key result is, an $O(1)$ transition(almost) can be observed with $\log(N)$ time evolution, i.e. $O(1)$ attention difference can be recognized.
